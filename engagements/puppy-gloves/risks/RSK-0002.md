## Trigger

An order requests a chargeable entity add or replace and the fee was not passed to the customer

## Mitigation

The orchestration flow stops and raises a manual approval task before any chargeable entity add or replace is sent to METCO, so nothing chargeable goes out automatically (vendor proposal, agreed by Martin Vasquez and Elena Marchetti)
