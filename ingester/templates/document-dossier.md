## How to review

This dossier holds what one section of a document adds to the register. It has two parts.

**Links** sections list rows that say what a held item or claim already says. Each row adds the document citation to the target's Source (Raised by on an open item, evidence on a claim) with a History line, and changes nothing else. Read the table, then accept it in bulk by writing `Bulk accept: <your name>` under the heading, or give a row its own Verdict of Accept or Reject. A row that is wrong, because the target says something different, is rejected here and raised as an item instead.

**Episodes** hold item blocks exactly as in a transcript dossier: new items, mutations of held items (Target is the existing id), questions and open items. They are reviewed the same way: a Verdict of Accept, Edit or Reject on every item graded Needs a human, and a bulk accept per episode for the Confident ones. Contradictions with the register are graded Needs a human.

When every verdict is filled, the skill writes Completed by and Completed on, and D3 writes this section.
