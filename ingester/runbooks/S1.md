# Runbook S1: Read

Version 0.7, 23 September 2026.

## Purpose

Read the whole transcript once, produce every candidate item with its evidence and grade, check the output, and present a dossier organised the way a person remembers the session: by episode.

## Inputs

- The signed session sheet, with every verdict filled.
- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.utterances.tsv`.
- `ingester/extraction-rules.md`.
- The engagement's item files: `stakeholders/`, `engagement.md`, `systems/`, `processes/`, `requirements/`, `decisions/`, `limitations/`, `risks/`, `open-items/` and `topics/`, read as context and as targets (R17). The tables under `index/` are a quick overview regenerated by S3; the item files are the truth.
- Earlier dossiers under `sessions/*/`, for their Questions for the SMEs.

## Command

```
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Tnnn S1
```

first writes one `stakeholders/STK-nnnn.md` per accepted New stakeholders row (through `ingester/bin/s1-stakeholders`, once per transcript) and then prints `S1 is performed by the skill; gate open` when the sheet is signed, or the reason it is not. The register is therefore current from the moment the sheet is signed, and a second transcript started before this one is written already sees these people. The reading itself is the skill: `/ingest-transcript Tnnn` from inside the engagement folder. A person doing it by hand follows the same procedure.

## Procedure

### 1. Read

In this order: `ingester/extraction-rules.md` in full; the signed session sheet; every file under `stakeholders/`; `engagement.md` (glossary and Phases); every file under `systems/` and `processes/`; every file under `requirements/`, `decisions/`, `limitations/`, `risks/` and `open-items/`; every file under `topics/`; then the whole utterance table end to end.

**The outstanding set.** A follow-up session changes the register as much as it adds to it. Before segmenting, list from the item files everything still open (R17): open items not Closed, risks not Realised or Retired, decisions in Proposed, requirements in Draft, limitations in Identified or Under assessment, claims Hedged or Contested, and every line under Questions for the SMEs in the earlier dossiers. Keep that list beside you while reading. Each member the session touches becomes a mutation item (step 4); each on-subject member the session never reaches goes under Closing, Outstanding not reached.

**The meeting subject as a prior.** Read `- Meeting subject:` from the session sheet before segmenting. When one is given: expect at least one episode on that subject and look for where the session reaches it; use its vocabulary to name and bound those episodes and to read noisily transcribed terms (a product name garbled by the transcriber is most likely a term from the subject); match those episodes first against existing topics whose title shares that vocabulary; set each episode's Subject to On subject, Related or Off subject. Episodes on other subjects are read, segmented, matched and graded exactly as if no subject had been given. If the session never reaches the subject, say so under Closing, Subject not reached. When no subject is given, infer the session's purpose from the opening minutes and the title, write it in `- Inferred purpose:`, and leave Subject blank on every episode.

### 2. Exchanges

Tag every utterance with one speech act and group utterances into exchanges with an outcome (design 5.2). Write `Tnnn.exchanges.md` (versioned). Part 1: one line per utterance, `utterance | speaker | speech act`, with the act one of ask, assert, propose, restate, accept, challenge, defer, hedge, aside. Part 2: one block per exchange with opening and closing utterances, participants, outcome (Answered and accepted, Answered and challenged, Deferred, Hedged, Unanswered) and the item numbers it yields. This is working, not reviewed, and it is what the scorer uses to tag failures.

### 3. Episodes

Group exchanges into episodes (design 5.3). Each episode has an ID `Tnnn-Enn`, a Title, a Span (first and last utterance and timestamps), a Topic (a TOP id or `new`), a Subject (On subject, Related, Off subject, or blank when no subject was given) and an Outcome (Settled, Parked, Unsettled, Informational, Aside). Greetings, scheduling, screen-share trouble and jokes are Aside episodes with no items (R9).

These are the episode headings of the dossier, written in step 4. `Tnnn.episodes.md` is not written by hand: after step 4, generate it with

```
ingester/bin/dossier2episodes sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md
```

which reads the headings back out and adds the item numbers each episode owns. The file repeated what the dossier already said, so the two could disagree; generating it means they cannot.

### 4. Dossier

Write `Tnnn.dossier.md` from `ingester/templates/dossier.md`, organised by episode, every item in the F4 block form shown in the template. For each exchange apply the rules:

- Present tense about today is never a REQ (R1). A statement about what a system does, holds or cannot do is a SYS.fact. How work is done, the steps of a process and statements about a process, is not extracted here: the dossier carries no PRC, PRC.step or PRC.fact item, and runbook S4 builds processes from the whole session after S3. A member of the outstanding set that is a process claim is left to S4 as well. A requirement that changes or keeps a step already in `processes/` links to it as usual; one about a process not yet recorded names the process in its Gist, and S4 adds the link. Confidence from standing: Stated, Second-hand, Hedged or Contested (R3, R4, R7). Legacy language is a Retired claim and nothing else (R5). "We do X but we don't need it" is Current, not needed with the reason quoted (R6).
- Commitment language about the solution from an Internal architect or SME is a REQ in Draft (R2). Its Phase is a name from the Phases list in `engagement.md`: the current phase unless the speakers place it later ("day two", "next release"), in which case the later phase named there. A vendor's "we need to support" is a DEC in Proposed raised by the vendor, or an OI (R2, R12).
- A vendor describing how their build will behave is a DEC in Proposed. It is Accepted only when an internal party whose Decides covers the subject accepted it in the exchange, and then it is graded Needs a human: authority check (R12, R19).
- A vendor Cannot fact about their own platform that blocks a stated need also yields a LIM with Identified on = session date (R7).
- An explicit deferral is an OI. The named party or role goes in the Gist, and is proposed as the Owner when it resolves to someone in the stakeholder register (R8). A hedge is a Hedged claim plus a Question (R3). An unsettled challenge is Contested claims on both sides plus a Question (R4).
- A restatement by an architect or consultant is cited `restated` and the claim is asserted by the confirmer; unconfirmed it is a Question (R13).
- One item per assertion; an exchange carrying a fact and a deferral yields two items with overlapping citations (R11). Read kind, confidence and outcome from the whole exchange (R14).
- Before proposing any item, check the engagement's existing claims, items and topics. A match sets `Target` to the existing id and proposes the update. A conflict is Needs a human: conflicts with an existing claim or item, naming both ids. A closure of an existing open item is an item of kind `OI` with `Target` the existing id and Status Closed (R17).
- A mutation item carries only the fields that change, each as it should read afterwards, plus the citations that justify the change. Its Gist states every change as `Field: old to new` so the reviewer judges it without opening the file. The moves table under R17 in `extraction-rules.md` says what each move needs; a move that needs a companion record (supersede a decision, realise a risk, disposition a limitation) is two items in the same dossier, the new one referred to as `item nn` in Links, Resolution or Disposition record. Never reopen a Closed open item and never change an Accepted decision except to Superseded; the integrity check refuses both.
- A speaker who reveals new standing yields an `STK.edit` item with a citation (R18). Each episode yields a `TOP` item naming the matched topic or proposing a new one.
- Propose a value for every field the register model requires, including the two the reviewer used to fill. An open item's Owner is the person who raised it, or whose Decides covers the subject; it must be a name in the stakeholder register, and never a person whose Role is Mentioned, because I19 rejects both. A requirement's MoSCoW is read from the strength of the commitment language: "needs to be one of the key features we get built" is Must. Say in the Gist that the value is a proposal, and grade the item Confident. Due stays blank, because I4 only warns on it. Leaving Owner or MoSCoW blank is not the cautious choice: I2 and I3 fail the whole dossier at S3, and R17 makes changing a wrong Owner later a one-line mutation.
- Grade every item with the conditions in `extraction-rules.md`, Grading conditions. Use exactly one reason from the controlled list.
- For a REQ, add `- Links: preserves SYS-nnn.fN` or `replaces ...` when it keeps or changes a claim in the record or in this dossier (refer to a claim proposed in this dossier by its item number, `item 07`, and S3 resolves it).

**Citation labels are not the speech act names.** Step 2 tags each utterance with a speech act; an F2 citation uses the label, and the two lists differ. `assert` becomes `answered`, which is the one nobody guesses.

| Speech act (step 2) | F2 label |
|---|---|
| ask | asked |
| assert | answered |
| propose | proposed |
| restate | restated |
| accept | accepted |
| challenge | challenged |
| defer | deferred |
| hedge | hedged |
| aside | aside |

Build each quote with `ingester/bin/quote <engagement> Tnnn <utt>:<lo>-<hi>` and copy its output, rather than retyping from the transcript. It parses the range exactly as `check-citations` does, so a quote cannot run past the range it cites, and it refuses a range spanning two speakers (R10).

Number items from 01 in dossier order. Set `- Session date:` and `- Meeting subject:` from the session sheet and `- Inferred purpose:` to the purpose read from the opening minutes when no subject was given, or to how the session related to the subject when one was.

### 5. Closing

Fill Closing: Questions for the SMEs (every Question item, one line each); Empty episodes (every Settled, Parked or Unsettled episode that yielded nothing, R15); Outstanding not reached (every on-subject member of the outstanding set the session did not touch, one line each with its id and title); Standing extensions proposed; Subject not reached (when a subject was given and no episode is On subject).

**A hedged claim is complete work, not pending work.** A claim recorded as Hedged or Contested, and a Question, is finished the moment it is written: the passage is kept, the uncertainty is recorded honestly, and nothing enters the current state as agreed. Neither asks the reviewer for a verdict, because the reviewer cannot resolve the hedge from the transcript either. R17 clears them, by building the next session's outstanding set from claims that are Hedged or Contested and from every line under Questions for the SMEs in earlier dossiers.

Two consequences worth knowing:

- **A Question writes nothing.** `s3-write` has `Question) ;;`, a deliberate no-op. A Question survives only as its line under Questions for the SMEs, which is where R17 reads it from. Accepting one carries it forward; it creates no record. A Question that should become an open item is not given a verdict saying so: change its kind to `OI` in the item heading, fill the fields an OI needs, and write `Edit`.
- **Hedged claims reach the reviewer through `index/outstanding.md` section 6**, not through the item files alone.

### 6. Self-checks

Run each, fix what can be fixed, rerun, and record what remains under Closing.

1. `ingester/bin/check-citations <engagement> Tnnn sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md` must print OK. Record the line under Citation check.
2. `ingester/bin/check-integrity <engagement> --proposed sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md`. Every failure here is the reader's to fix, now that Owner and MoSCoW are proposed in step 4: a missing Raised by, an Implemented by, a Status not in the model, an `STK.edit` whose Target is not an existing `STK-nnnn` (I20). Anything that genuinely cannot be resolved from the transcript is listed under Integrity check with its item number and the reason. Record the last line under Integrity check.
3. No item may carry `Confidence: Hedged` or `Confidence: Contested` with `Grade: Confident`. This is the failure R16 exists to prevent, and it is mechanical:

   ```
   ingester/bin/dossier2tsv sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md |
     awk -F'\t' '$3 == "Confident" && $8 ~ /Confidence=(Hedged|Contested)/ { print $1 }'
   ```

   It must print nothing. Any item it names is regraded `Needs a human` with the reason `hedged` or `contested`.
4. Every item has an `answered` or `proposed` citation, or is a Question.
5. No Confident item carries a hedge word in its content citation (probably, I think, my guess, maybe, I'm not sure, you'd have to ask).
6. Every episode that is Settled, Parked or Unsettled owns at least one item.

### 7. Accept the Confident items

Use the `- Bulk accept:` line the episode heading already carries. For every episode whose items are all graded Confident, write the reviewer's name on that line and leave the Verdict lines blank: `dossier_signed` counts a blank verdict on a Confident item in a bulk-accepted episode as filled, and `accepted_items` merges it. Only in an episode that mixes Confident and Needs a human items is `- Verdict: Accept` written on the Confident ones individually.

Leave every item graded Needs a human blank: those are the reviewer's. Add `### How the Confident items were accepted` at the end of Closing, recording that the verdicts were given by episode under this rule rather than marked one by one, and giving the episode and item counts. This does not sign the dossier; Completed by, Completed on and the Needs a human verdicts still gate S3.

### 8. Log and present

Write a run log with `ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Tnnn log S1 "<outcome>"`. Append to `LOG.md`: `- <today>: Tnnn S1 read; dossier v<n> with <items> items (<new> new, <mutations> changing existing ids), <needs> needing a human; awaiting review.`

**The reviewer does not scroll the dossier.** A two hour session yields a couple of hundred items and a handful that need judgement; presenting all of them buries the handful. Present, in this order and nothing else:

1. Every item graded Needs a human, in full, with its evidence and the reason it was graded so. This is the reviewer's whole job.
2. One line giving how many hedged and contested claims and Questions were accepted as drafted, and saying that R17 raises them next session.
3. One line per proposed Owner and MoSCoW, in the form `item nn: Owner Martin Vasquez (proposed)`, so a wrong one is corrected in a word.
4. The counts (items, new, mutations, needing a human, outstanding not reached), the episodes bulk accepted, and the dossier path.

Everything else is in the dossier for the record and is never read aloud. Then stop.

## Outputs

- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.exchanges.md`: a working file, kept for the reviewer and the scorer, not reviewed on its own.
- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.episodes.md`: generated from the dossier by `ingester/bin/dossier2episodes`, so the two cannot disagree. Never edited by hand.
- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md`: the review file.
- A run log under `logs/`.

## Automatic checks

The self-checks in step 6.

## What the reviewer does

Nothing at S1. The reviewer's work is S2.

## What approval means

Not applicable. S1 ends when the dossier is presented.

## On rejection

When the reviewer has written `Reject` on the dossier header's Completed by line or asked for a re-read, read their notes under Closing, produce `Tnnn.dossier.v2.md` with `- Dossier version: 2`, and leave the rejected file untouched.
