# Runbook S1: Read

Version 0.1, 10 September 2026.

## Purpose

Read the whole transcript once, produce every candidate item with its evidence and grade, check the output, and present a dossier organised the way a person remembers the session: by episode.

## Inputs

- The signed session sheet.
- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.utterances.tsv`.
- `ingester/extraction-rules.md`.
- The engagement's `stakeholders.md`, `engagement.md`, `current-state-*.md`, `registers/*.md` and `topics.md`, read as context and as targets (R17).

## Command

```
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Tnnn S1
```

prints `S1 is performed by the skill; gate open` when the sheet is signed, or the reason it is not. The reading itself is the skill: `/ingest-transcript Tnnn <engagement>`. A person doing it by hand follows this order:

1. Read the rules file in full, then the sheet, then the engagement files, then the utterance table end to end.
2. Tag every utterance with one speech act and group utterances into exchanges with an outcome (design 5.2). Write `Tnnn.exchanges.md`.
3. Group exchanges into episodes with title, span, topic, subject and outcome (design 5.3). Write `Tnnn.episodes.md`.
4. For every exchange, apply R1 to R19 to decide what it yields, check the engagement for matches and conflicts, attach citations by exchange part, grade the item, and write it as an F4 item block under its episode in `Tnnn.dossier.md` from `ingester/templates/dossier.md`.
5. Fill Closing.
6. Run the automatic checks below and fix what can be fixed.

## Outputs

- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.exchanges.md` and `Tnnn.episodes.md`: working files, kept for the reviewer and the scorer, not reviewed on their own.
- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md`: the review file.
- A run log under `logs/`.

## Automatic checks

- `ingester/bin/check-citations <engagement> Tnnn <dossier>` must print OK.
- `ingester/bin/check-integrity <engagement> --proposed <dossier>` on the accepted set merged with the engagement. Failures needing the reviewer are listed under Closing, Integrity check.
- Every item has an `answered` or `proposed` citation, or is a Question.
- No Confident item carries a hedge word in its content citation.
- Every Settled, Parked or Unsettled episode owns at least one item (R15).

## What the reviewer does

Nothing at S1. The reviewer's work is S2.

## What approval means

Not applicable. S1 ends when the dossier is presented.

## On rejection

Not applicable.
