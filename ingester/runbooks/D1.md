# Runbook D1: Read one section of a document

Version 0.2, 19 October 2026.

## Purpose

Read one section of a signed document against the register and the current-state record, and draft what it adds: links where it says what is already held, mutations or questions where it contradicts what is held, and new items where it says something new and its treatment lets it. One section at a time keeps a large workbook reviewable and inside one reading. This is the counterpart of S1, with the matching against the register made explicit because a document mostly covers known ground.

## Inputs

- The signed document sheet, and the stakeholder files written from it (`stage <engagement> Dnnn D1` writes them once, when the sheet is first signed).
- The fragment table rows of the section (`awk -F'\t' '$4 == "<Section>"' sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.fragments.tsv`).
- `ingester/extraction-rules.md`, including R28 to R30 for documents.
- The engagement's records: `index/*.md` to find candidates, then the item and element files themselves before linking to them.

## Command

```
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Dnnn status     names the next section: needs-d1:<slug>
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Dnnn check <slug>
```

Or `/ingest-document Dnnn`, which takes the next section in sheet order, or `/ingest-document Dnnn <slug>` for a named one.

## Procedure

1. Reads the section's rows in full, in unit order, with the header row so each cell is read under its column.
2. For each row, or each group of rows that says one thing, finds what the register and the record already hold on it, and classifies it (R30):
   - **Corroborates**: a held item or claim says the same. One row in the Links table (F18): `| <n> | <unit:fragment> | <target id> | <documented citation> | |`. The target is the most specific holder: a claim id (`SYS-0012.f3`) over its element, the item over a claim that only mentions it. A row may corroborate several targets; each is a row of its own.
   - **Contradicts**: a held item or claim says something the row denies or changes. An F4 mutation of the held id when the section's treatment gives standing to change it (Origin or Attributed with standing on the subject), graded Needs a human; otherwise a Question or OI to settle it with the person who holds the subject. A Corroborate-only section never mutates; a contradiction there is always a Question.
   - **New**: nothing held. An F4 new item, under the section's treatment: Origin rows with the author's standing, Attributed rows as Second-hand from the named person, with an OI to confirm with them unless the sheet records the collection as trusted (R29). A Corroborate-only section raises nothing new, except under a Notes exception the sheet names.
   - **Nothing**: headers, totals, ids, values with nothing to say. Not drafted.
3. Writes `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn-<slug>.dossier.md` from `templates/document-dossier.md`. Links go in one or more `## Dnnn-Lnn` sections, each with its Bulk accept line and table, grouped as the reviewer will read them (by target type, or by row range). New items, mutations and questions go in `## Dnnn-Enn` episodes, one per row or group of rows that belong together, with Span given as unit range and locations. Item blocks are F4 exactly as in a transcript dossier; every citation is a document citation built with `ingester/bin/quote`. Header counts: Rows read, Links, New items, Mutations.
4. Self-checks with `stage ... check <slug>` (citations and integrity on the proposed set, which includes I29: every Links target exists). Fixes and reruns until clean.
5. Presents the dossier in the terminal: the counts, the Links sections as a summary (target type and number of rows), then every item graded Needs a human in full, then the path. Then it stops.

## Outputs

- `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn-<slug>.dossier.md`.
- A run log under `logs/`.

## Automatic checks

- `check-citations` on the dossier (the author must be the sheet's Author, the location the first fragment's).
- `check-integrity --proposed` on the dossier: the I-rules on new items and mutations as for a transcript, and I29 on Links rows.

## What the reviewer does

Reads the Links tables and bulk accepts each, or rejects single rows that link to the wrong target. Gives every item graded Needs a human a Verdict, and bulk accepts the Confident ones per episode, as in a transcript dossier (runbook S2). The skill writes Completed by and Completed on on the reviewer's word.

## What approval means

Each accepted link adds the document's citation to its target and changes nothing else. Each accepted item is written as drafted or edited.

## What rejection means

A rejected link adds nothing. A rejected item writes nothing; when it was wrong because the row says something else, the reviewer adds the right item as a new block.
