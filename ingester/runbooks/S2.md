# Runbook S2: Review

Version 0.3, 14 September 2026.

## Purpose

The human judges the dossier. Every item that needs judgement gets one; items the method is confident about are read and may be accepted in bulk. This is the only place human effort is spent, and design Q4 measures it.

## Inputs

- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.dossier.md`.
- The utterance table, for checking any quote in context.

## Command

None. Open the dossier in an editor.

## Outputs

The same dossier with a Verdict on every item, edits made in place, and a signed header.

## Automatic checks

- `ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Tnnn status` prints `needs-s3` when the dossier passes the gate and `awaiting-dossier` otherwise. `ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Tnnn check` reruns citations and integrity on the edited dossier.

## What the reviewer does

Read every episode. Then:

- Items graded **Needs a human** each get `Accept`, `Edit` or `Reject` on their Verdict line. To edit, change the field lines in place and write `Edit`. The reason on the grade says what to look at.
- Items graded **Confident** may be accepted one by one, or in bulk for an episode by writing your name on the episode's `Bulk accept:` line after reading them all. A blank verdict on a Confident item in a bulk-accepted episode counts as Accept. A Confident item you disagree with gets `Edit` or `Reject` individually; that disagreement is counted as Confident-but-wrong and tightens the grading rule. The skill accepts the Confident items for you when it writes the dossier and says so; your attention is meant for the Needs a human list.
- **Questions** get an answer written on the Gist line and Verdict `Accept`, or Verdict `raise an OI` and the fields an OI needs.
- **Missed passages** are added as new items in the F4 shape, numbered after the last item, with citations. They are scored as Missed against the draft.
- Fields the reviewer always fills: Owner and MoSCoW where the item lacks them.
- Integrity failures listed under Closing are fixed by editing the item, or waived with a reason written beside the entry.

Then the dossier is complete (see Completing the dossier below).

## What approval means

Every item reads as true to the transcript in the reviewer's judgement, every question has a disposition, and integrity failures I1 to I14 and I17 to I19 are zero or explicitly waived. Silence is not approval: a blank verdict outside a bulk-accepted episode blocks S3.

## On rejection

To reject the dossier outright, write `Reject` on the Completed by line and your notes under Closing. The skill re-reads with the notes as context and writes `Tnnn.dossier.v2.md`; the rejected file is kept.

## Completing the dossier

A dossier is complete when every Verdict is filled and both automatic checks pass. There is no separate approval step and nobody stops to ask for one. A Confident item in an episode carrying `Bulk accept:` counts as filled.

When `stage` says `awaiting-dossier`, the skill reads the dossier and checks every Verdict.

- **Any Verdict blank.** The outstanding items are presented in the terminal, grouped by the reason each was graded Needs a human, one line each saying what is needed to fill it, including any field still missing such as an Owner or a MoSCoW. "Accept all" plus any corrections completes the dossier and starts S3.
- **Every Verdict filled.** `ingester/bin/check-citations` and `ingester/bin/check-integrity <engagement> --proposed` are run on the dossier and what can be fixed is fixed. `- Completed by:` is written with the reviewer's name and `- Completed on:` with today's date into the header, a short `### How this dossier was completed` section is added at the end of Closing recording the rule and where the verdicts came from, and S3 follows in the same turn.

A dossier is never marked complete while a Verdict is blank, and a verdict is never invented to get there.
