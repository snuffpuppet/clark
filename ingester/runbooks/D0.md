# Runbook D0: Prepare a document

Version 0.2, 19 October 2026.

## Purpose

Turn a document as received (xlsx, pptx or pdf) into the fragment table that every document citation points at, and produce the document sheet that fixes who the document speaks as, with what standing, and how each of its sections is to be read. This is the counterpart of S0 for a transcript. Every later stage depends on the treatments confirmed here.

## Inputs

- The document file, either a path outside the engagement (first invocation) or already registered in `engagements/<name>/documents/` and sitting in `documents/unprocessed/`.
- Every file under `stakeholders/`, and `engagement.md`, Who makes requirements and decisions.

## Command

```
ingester/bin/register-document <engagement> <file> ["title"]
make -C ingester flatten ENG=<engagement> DOC=Dnnn
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Dnnn D0
```

Or, as the skill from inside the engagement folder: `/ingest-document <file> ["title"]`. The flattener runs in the ingester's Docker image (`ingester/docker/`), which `make -C ingester build` builds once. It is the only part of the ingester that needs Docker.

## Procedure

`make flatten` writes `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.fragments.tsv` (F16), `Dnnn.sections.tsv` and `Dnnn.meta.tsv`. `stage ... D0` checks the SHA-256 and the table and writes `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.document-sheet.md` (F17) with one Sections row per sheet (xlsx), Slides and Notes (pptx) or Pages (pdf), and the Author proposed from the file's properties. The reader (the skill, or a person doing it by hand) then:

1. Reads enough of every section to say what it holds: its header row or first slides, a sample of rows, and any column that names people or sources. A section of a few hundred rows is sampled; nothing is drafted from it yet.
2. Fills the header. **Author** is the person or organisation the document speaks as, and every citation of the document names it, so it is set before any citation is written. It is usually the organisation that issued it ("Tech Mahindra"), not the person who last saved the file. **Organisation**, **Document date** (`D Month YYYY`, from the document's own version table or cover where it has one, otherwise the file properties), **Document version**, **Kind** (Vendor deliverable, ABB internal, Design document or Other) and **Domain**.
3. Proposes a **Treatment** for every section, with the reason under Notes for the reviewer:
   - **Origin** when the section is the author's own account. The author's standing then follows the rules for people under `engagement.md`: a vendor's statements about our systems are Second-hand, and its design intent is a DEC candidate in Proposed, never a decision.
   - **Attributed** when each row names the person it came from. Name the column under Attribution column (`WHO (B)`, `Comments (Y)`).
   - **Corroborate only** when the section renders ground the register already holds, above all a vendor's write-up of sessions already ingested. This is the playback rule applied to a document: what the vendor plays back is not new evidence.
   - **Skip** for cover sheets, version tables, list-value sheets, and hidden sheets populated from another section.

   A section can mix kinds of row. Say so in Notes and name the rows that differ, by a column value where there is one (for example, rows whose Validation Status is Previously Captured are the vendor's own historical baseline, Origin, inside a section that is otherwise Corroborate only). D1 reads that note.

   For an Attributed section, ask whether the author's collection is trusted and record the answer under Notes. When it is, R29 raises no open item to confirm each attributed item; the Source citation is the record.
4. Proposes New stakeholders rows, in the session sheet's columns: Role **Author** for the people who wrote the document, **Attributed** for each person an Attributed section names who is not in the register, and **Mentioned** for owners or contacts named but not quoted. Proposed is `new` on every row. Passage is one document citation, built with `ingester/bin/quote`. For a long attribution column, list the distinct names with their row counts under Notes and propose rows for the people the register will need as Raised by; a stakeholder file is written only for an accepted Author or Attributed row.
5. Leaves every Verdict cell blank, and Approver and Approved on blank.
6. Runs `ingester/bin/check-citations <engagement> Dnnn sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.document-sheet.md`, fixes any FAIL and reruns until OK.
7. Appends to `LOG.md`: `- <today>: Dnnn D0 proposals written for <n> section(s) and <m> person(s); awaiting the document sheet.`
8. Presents the proposals in the terminal: the header, a table with one row per section (Section, Fragments, Treatment, Attribution column, reason in a few words), then the people table (Name, Role, Segment, Rows), then the sheet path. It says the verdicts can be given in the terminal and will be written into the sheet with the Approver and Approved on date on the reviewer's word. Then it stops.

## Outputs

- `documents/Dnnn.md` (register file), the document under `documents/unprocessed/`.
- `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.fragments.tsv`, `Dnnn.sections.tsv`, `Dnnn.meta.tsv`.
- `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.document-sheet.md`.
- A run log under `logs/`.

## Automatic checks

- `register-document` refuses a file already registered (same SHA-256), and a format other than xlsx, pptx or pdf.
- The flattener and `d0-prepare` check the SHA-256 against `documents/Dnnn.md`. A mismatch blocks.
- `d0-prepare` refuses a fragment table without the F16 header, with a malformed row, or with a repeated `unit:fragment` key, and refuses to overwrite a sheet.
- `check-citations` passes on the sheet before it is presented.

## What the reviewer does

Confirm the header, Author above all. Give each section a Verdict of Accept, Edit (after changing its Treatment or Attribution column in place) or Reject (read as Skip). Give each person Accept, Edit or Reject. Then sign with Approver and Approved on. All of it can be done in conversation.

## What approval means

The document speaks as the Author with the standing the sheet gives it, section by section, and D1 reads each section under its Treatment and nothing else.

## What rejection means

A section rejected is not read. A sheet that is wrong as a whole is deleted with its fragment table and D0 run again; nothing has been written to the register.
