# Runbook S0: Prepare

Version 0.3, 10 September 2026.

## Purpose

Turn the transcript file as received into the utterance table that every citation points at, and produce the session sheet that fixes who spoke, in what role, with what standing and decision authority. Every downstream rule depends on the roles confirmed here (design 4.6, R18).

## Inputs

- The VTT file, either a path outside the repository (first invocation) or already registered in `engagements/<name>/transcripts.md` and sitting in `transcripts/unprocessed/`.
- Every file under `engagements/<name>/stakeholders/`.
- Optionally the meeting subject.

## Command

First invocation for a transcript:

```
ingester/bin/register-transcript <engagement> <path-to-vtt> ["meeting subject"]
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Tnnn S0
```

Or, as the skill: `/ingest-transcript <path-to-vtt> <engagement> ["meeting subject"]`. The skill runs both commands and then fills the role proposals.

## Outputs

- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.utterances.tsv`: one row per cue, sorted by utterance then fragment (format F1).
- `sessions/Tnnn/Tnnn.session.md`: header, Attendees table with each speaker matched to a stakeholder file or marked `new`, New stakeholders table with the skill's proposed role, segment, department, standing and Decides for each new speaker and for people named but absent, one citation each.
- A run log under `logs/`.

## Automatic checks

- The file's SHA-256 matches the transcript register. A mismatch blocks.
- `stage` refuses S0 when the sheet already exists.
- The skill runs `ingester/bin/check-citations` on the sheet before presenting it.

## What the reviewer does

Confirm the Session date and Domain. The skill presents every proposal as a table in the terminal, so this can all be done in conversation: say which rows are Correct or Accept and which need a change, and the skill writes the verdicts, the Approver and the Approved on date into the sheet. Editing the sheet by hand does the same job. For each attendee, Verdict is Correct or Edit, confirming the speaker is who the STK column says; a correction to an existing stakeholder's role or standing goes through an STK.edit item in the dossier under R18, not through this table. For each proposed stakeholder, write Accept, Edit or Reject. Segment is Residential, BE&G or Wholesale when the person speaks for one customer segment and blank when they speak for the whole business; Department is where they sit. Decides is the field that lets an SME's acceptance make a decision Accepted (R19), so it deserves care: normally the SME's area of expertise; blank for architects and vendors; for a Forum row, what that body approves.

## What approval means

The roles, standing and decision authority are right for everyone who spoke, and the stakeholder register is complete for this session. A blank verdict is pending and blocks S1.

## On rejection

There is nothing to reject: the sheet is the register's proposal. Edit it until it is right and sign. To start S0 again, delete the session sheet by hand and rerun; the utterance table is regenerated from the unchanged transcript.
