# Runbook D3: Write one section of a document

Version 0.1, 19 October 2026.

## Purpose

Write the signed dossier of one section into the register, the record and the topic ledger, as S3 does for a transcript, and move the document to `documents/processed/` once its last section is written.

## Inputs

- The signed document sheet and the signed section dossier `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn-<slug>.dossier.md`.

## Command

```
ingester/bin/stage <engagement> Dnnn D3 <slug>
```

which runs `ingester/bin/s3-write <engagement> Dnnn --document <slug>`.

## Procedure

`s3-write --document` does all of it, or nothing:

1. Checks the gates: the sheet is signed, the section is on it and not Skip or Rejected, the dossier names the same Slug and is signed, and the session log has no `- Section <slug> written on:` line.
2. Checks every accepted citation and the integrity of the proposed set, as S3 does.
3. Writes the stakeholder files from the sheet if D1 has not (once per document).
4. Writes new items, mutations with History lines, claims, topics and stakeholder edits exactly as S3. A new process step in a document dossier is refused; processes are built at S4 from a transcript.
5. Writes each accepted Links row: its citation is appended to the target's Source (Raised by on an open item, `evidence` on a claim) with a History line `D Month YYYY | Dnnn link Ln | corroborated by the document, no field changed | <citation>`. Status, confidence and every other field are left as they are (R30).
6. Records in the session log `- Section <slug> completed by:` and `- Section <slug> written on:`, one row per id created, updated or linked, and in `documents/Dnnn.md` the sheet's header fields and the slug under `sections-written`.
7. Renders the index, including `index/documents.md`.
8. When every section the sheet does not Skip is written, moves the document to `documents/processed/` and checks its SHA-256 there.

On any failure the engagement is restored to its state before the write, as for S3.

## Outputs

- Item, claim, topic and stakeholder files; `documents/Dnnn.md`; `sessions/Dnnn/Dnnn.session-log.md`; `index/`.
- A run log under `logs/`; a line in `LOG.md`.

## Automatic checks

As listed under Procedure, steps 1 and 2.

## What the reviewer does

Nothing at this stage. The skill reports the counts and the sections still to write.

## What approval means

Signing the dossier at D1 is the approval; D3 has no gate of its own.

## What rejection means

Not applicable. To undo a written section, edit the files by hand with History lines; git is the backup.
